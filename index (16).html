Picture it

Your five hours

The doors close behind you and the house is yours. Glasses of alcohol free sparkling wine go round beneath the chandeliers, and the music’s already playing. One by one, your people slip away for a private reading with me and come back with something to say about it. Someone’s on the mic. Someone’s at the sober bar ordering another coffee. Someone’s quietly building a dream board for the year they want. Five hours later, everyone walks out with a gift bag and a story about the night.

What’s yours for five hours

One house. Only your people.

The whole house

Every room, every chandelier, the Victorian balcony. No other bookings, no strangers, nobody asking to share your table. From the moment you arrive, it’s your Clubhouse.

Private readings

One to one with me, in private. A full 45 minutes for the guest of honour, 20 minutes for everyone else on a reading ticket.

Karaoke and music

The music’s on from the start and the mic is yours all event long, for the one who’s been waiting all year to do it.

The dream board session

For anyone who wants one, a guided session on building a dream board and the psychology and neurology behind why people use them. Everything’s provided, and your board goes home with you.

Plus a welcome glass of alcohol free sparkling wine for every guest, barista service throughout, a gift bag with a crystal and sage, and the sober bar for anything else you fancy.

The readings

Private, personal, yours alone

I start the moment your event begins. One by one, each guest on a reading ticket joins me in private. Your cards are shuffled in front of you, for you, and the reading is yours alone, never a show for the room. While each person is with me, everyone else carries on with the party.

Questions

Before you book

How long is it?

Five hours, start to finish.

Who’s it for?

Birthdays, hen parties, milestones, mixed groups. Any group with something to celebrate.

What if fewer than 8 want a reading?

The minimum is 8 reading tickets per booking. Everyone else can come on a guest ticket.

What’s at the sober bar?

Everyone gets a welcome glass of alcohol free sparkling wine and barista service throughout. Anything else from the sober bar can be bought on the day.

Is food included?

No, food isn’t included.

What if we need to cancel?

The deposit isn’t refunded if you cancel.

Where is it?

Marie Monroe’s Clubhouse, Winpenny House, 45 High Street, Stockton-on-Tees.
