Booking terms

The Private Clubhouse Experience

Last updated 12 November 2026

These terms apply to every booking of The Private Clubhouse Experience at Marie Monroe’s Clubhouse, Winpenny House, 45 High Street, Stockton-on-Tees. By paying your deposit, you agree to them.

1. Your booking

  1. The Private Clubhouse Experience runs on Sundays only, with one private booking each Sunday.
  2. Your booking is for five hours, starting at the time you choose when you book. The five hours includes everything.
  3. You have exclusive use of the Clubhouse for your group for the duration of your booking.

2. Tickets and numbers

  1. Reading tickets are £129 per person and include a private reading: 45 minutes for the organiser or birthday person, and 20 minutes for every other reading guest.
  2. Every booking must include a minimum of 8 and a maximum of 10 reading tickets.
  3. Guest tickets, without a reading, are £89 per person.
  4. Readings take place one after another, in private, starting when your event begins.

3. Deposit

  1. A £250 deposit is required to secure your Sunday. Your date is not reserved until the deposit has been paid.
  2. The deposit is deducted from your final bill.
  3. The deposit is non-refundable if you cancel your booking.

4. Final numbers and payment

  1. Once you confirm your final numbers of reading tickets and guest tickets, we will send you an invoice for the balance, minus your deposit.
  2. Your final numbers and full payment of the balance must both be received no later than 14 days before your event.
  3. You will be charged for the numbers you confirm, with a minimum of 8 reading tickets, even if fewer people attend on the day.

5. What’s included

  1. Your booking includes karaoke and music, the guided dream board session, a welcome glass of alcohol free sparkling wine for each guest, barista service, and a gift bag with a crystal and sage.
  2. Food is not included.
  3. Any other drinks from the sober bar are paid for on the day.
  4. The Clubhouse is an alcohol free venue.

6. Your cancellation rights

  1. Because this is a booking for a leisure activity on a specific date, the 14 day cooling off period under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 does not apply.
  2. This does not affect your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
