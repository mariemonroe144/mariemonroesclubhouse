Not somewhere you visit. Somewhere you belong.

Questions

Before you join

What exactly is Marie Monroe’s Clubhouse?

A private members’ club at Winpenny House on Stockton High Street. A whole social life without the alcohol: wellbeing, social nights, nightlife and an upstairs members’ space, all under one roof.

What’s included in membership?

Depending on your membership, the weekly timetable, the balcony bar and upstairs members’ space, priority entry, and member prices on special events.

What’s the difference between Club, Day and Evening?

Club is everything, £75 a month. Evening is every evening event from 7pm, £50 a month. Day is every daytime class, including morning and after work meditation, £50 a month.

How does the Founding 25 offer work?

The first 25 members pay £60 a month, locked in for life, for the whole weekly timetable, unlimited meditation, the balcony bar and upstairs whenever they like, priority entry, member prices on special events, no joining fee, and their photo and name on the founders’ wall.

Is there a joining fee?

A one off £25 joining fee applies to Day, Evening and Club memberships. It’s waived for the founding 25.

What happens when membership is full?

Membership is limited. Once every place is taken, new applicants join the waiting list.

What happens if an event is full?

The house holds a maximum of 60 guests at once. Members always have priority entry over non members.

Where is the Clubhouse?

Winpenny House, 45 High Street, Stockton-on-Tees. Opposite the fountains, next to the new park.

How do I apply?

Fill in the application form. We’ll be in touch personally to confirm your place, or add you to the waiting list once we’re full.

When does my membership begin?

Once we’ve been in touch and confirmed your place with you.
